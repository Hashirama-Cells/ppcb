## 1. What the Provider does

The Provider sets up and runs LeadBot for the Client. LeadBot:
- replies by text message to new enquiries from the Client's website form, lead ads or business text number
- answers basic questions using information the Client has approved
- offers open appointment times and books them
- alerts the Client by text when a job is booked, or when a customer needs a person (for example an emergency, a complaint, or a request to speak to someone)

The Client gets a dashboard showing leads and bookings.

## 2. What the Client does

The Client agrees to:
- give accurate information about services, prices, hours and service area, and tell the Provider when it changes
- keep the text-message consent wording the Provider supplies on any form that sends leads to LeadBot
- only send LeadBot leads from people who asked to be contacted
- follow up promptly on leads LeadBot passes to the Client
- complete any phone carrier registration needed for business texting (the Provider will help)

## 3. Fees

- **Setup fee:** $[750], paid before setup begins.
- **Monthly fee:** $[297] per month, starting on the day LeadBot goes live. It's charged automatically by card through Stripe.
- Text message and AI usage are included up to normal use for a business of the Client's size. If usage becomes unusually high, the Provider will discuss it with the Client before charging anything extra.

## 4. Guarantee

If LeadBot does not book at least one appointment in the first 30 days after going live, the Provider will refund the first monthly fee on request. The setup fee is [refundable / not refundable].

## 5. Length and cancelling

This agreement runs month to month. Either side can cancel at any time by email or text. Service continues until the end of the month already paid for, and there are no cancellation fees.

## 6. No promise of results

The Provider will do its best to keep LeadBot running and working well, but can't promise a specific number of leads, bookings or sales. LeadBot relies on outside services (hosting, phone carriers, AI providers) that may occasionally be unavailable. The Provider will fix problems it can control within [2] business days of hearing about them.

## 7. Customer information

Information about the Client's customers belongs to the Client. The Provider will:
- use it only to run LeadBot for the Client
- not sell or share it
- delete it within 30 days after this agreement ends, or send the Client a copy first if asked

## 8. Liability

Neither side is responsible for indirect losses, such as lost profits. The Provider's total responsibility under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client paid in the 3 months before the problem.
