## Step 3: the message

Send it by email, through their website contact form, or as a Facebook page message. Fill in the [brackets]. Use version A if they were slow, and version B if they were fast or you skipped the test.

**Version A: they were slow**

> Hi, my name is [your name]. I'm local, from St. Pauls.
>
> On [day] I sent a question through your website as a test, and the first reply came [X hours later / the next day]. Most homeowners with a leak call the next roofer on Google if they don't hear back in a few minutes.
>
> I set up a tool for local contractors that texts every new enquiry back within seconds, answers basic questions and books the inspection, day or night. I made a version for [business name] you can try as if you were a customer; it takes about a minute:
> [demo link]
>
> If it looks useful, I'd be glad to show you in 10 minutes, at your office or by phone.
>
> [your name]
> [your phone]
> [your mailing address]

**Version B: they were fast, or you didn't test**

> Hi, my name is [your name], I'm local from St. Pauls.
>
> I set up a tool for local roofers that texts back every website enquiry within seconds, nights and weekends included, answers basic questions and books the inspection. I made a version for [business name] you can try as a customer; it takes about a minute:
> [demo link]
>
> Would it be worth a 10-minute look? Happy to stop by or call.
>
> [your name]
> [your phone]
> [your mailing address]

**Follow-up (3 days later, if no answer)**

> Hi, just checking you saw this. Here's the demo again: [demo link]. Happy to answer any questions. [your name]

Send each message one at a time, never as a bulk email. If anyone says "not interested", thank them and stop.
